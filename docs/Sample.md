# Hanko sample

This note shows the building blocks of the theme. It carries `cssclasses: seal`, so the seal sits at the top right: the note is signed off.

## Bold

Bold text is set in the **anchor colour**: **Burnt Sienna** in light mode, **Raw Sienna** in dark mode. Headings stay ink.

## Role callouts

> [!recall] Self-test
> Retrieve knowledge. Raised, because you answer here.

> [!review] Review
> What happened last week?

> [!opinion] Opinion
> There is no right answer.

> [!ask] Question to me
> A message to myself.

> [!correct] Correct
> Confirmation.

> [!upfront] Upfront question
> My turn, before I know the answer.

> [!answer] Answer
> The answer to the upfront question.

> [!exercise] Exercise, 10 min
> Timed work, in ink.

## Callout modifiers

> [!opinion|no-title]
> Without a title line.

> [!ask|no-icon] Without an icon
> Written as `[!ask|no-icon]`.

> [!recall|plain] Flat
> No card, only the line in the role colour.

## Evidence and status tags
